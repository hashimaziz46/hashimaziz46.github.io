## Problem

Search Acumen works in legal tech and conveyancing. Manual review of land registry documents across 145 land authorities was a major bottleneck in their conveyancing workflows.

## My role

Project Manager for a 10-person team over a 6-month engagement, delivered through Geeks Ltd.

## What I did

- Led design and delivery of an AI-powered document processing system (OpenAI GPT-4, later migrated to Claude, plus OCR)
- Automated processing coverage across all 145 land authorities
- Established an AI-assisted QA process with automated error detection
- Managed a structured offboarding process at acquisition - full documentation and code handover, zero delays

## Outcome

- 87% reduction in document processing time - from ~2 hours down to under 15 minutes
- 80% improvement in overall processing throughput
- 75% reduction in reported issues
- $660K in revenue over the 6-month engagement

> From ~2 hours to under 15 minutes per document, across all 145 land authorities.
