## Problem

Toyota's leasing department (automotive finance) needed a comprehensive automation programme - correspondence, invoicing and reporting - delivered across time zones without losing cadence.

## My role

Project Manager for a 60-person distributed team: 20 onshore in the US and 40 offshore, delivered through CareCloud.

## What I did

- Led the 60-person distributed team across onshore and offshore, maintaining delivery cadence across time zones
- Automated 6 categories of lease correspondence and invoice generation, with dynamic tax-rate updates
- Built an internal reporting dashboard and a QA department with internal tooling support
- Defined an ad hoc request-handling process, and presented quarterly business reviews to C-suite and VP stakeholders

## Outcome

- 80% reduction in invoicing errors and 60% increase in processing efficiency
- 30%+ improvement in letter processing efficiency
- 70% faster error detection and 23% fewer overall incidents
- 1st place in Toyota's inter-departmental efficiency competition, with zero incidents over the 6-month judging period

> 60 people, two continents, one delivery cadence.
