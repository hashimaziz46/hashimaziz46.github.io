## Problem

After an acquisition, SOMOS (billing and telecom infrastructure) needed a full infrastructure transition to internal ownership - with zero operational disruption across a 30-person team.

## My role

Project Manager for a 30-person team over a 6-month transition, including 3 months of parallel running, delivered through CareCloud.

## What I did

- Managed the full post-acquisition transition over 6 months, ensuring zero disruption throughout
- Led automation of the invoice generation process
- Built a QA department from scratch, including test case templates and boilerplates
- Owned quarterly business reviews with C-suite and VP-level stakeholders

## Outcome

- 20% increase in billing efficiency
- 5% reduction in invoice and billing errors
- $3.7M in quarterly revenue under my direct PM ownership after the transition

> Six months, three of them in parallel run, and zero disruption.
