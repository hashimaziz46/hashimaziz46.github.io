## Problem

Shippeo runs a logistics and supply-chain SaaS. Their fleet platform needed real-time telematics - live driver tracking and shipment location - integrated into production without disrupting sprint delivery.

## My role

Project Manager for a 19-engineer team over a 10-month delivery in 2-week Agile sprints, delivered through GitStart.

## What I did

- Led backend integration of real-time telematics data, managing a cross-functional team of 19 engineers, QA and frontend developers
- Maintained a risk register to catch API mapping mismatches and invalid data formats before they hit sprint delivery
- Designed and rolled out a shared tracking dashboard for the team and the client
- Ran bi-weekly alignment sessions with the Tech Lead and client to resolve blockers early

## Outcome

- 2,500 telematics units shipped to production, a month ahead of the 11-month deadline
- $1.2M in client revenue contributed
- 15% faster response time to telematics status queries
- 7% improvement in overall delivery speed

> Delivered in 10 months against an 11-month deadline.
